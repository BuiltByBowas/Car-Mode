Driving

Click the car (or press Alt+Shift+C) for the quick menu: screenshot, reload, zoom, mini player, read aloud, dark page, translate and more.

Game mode: menu → Drive it (or Alt+Shift+D). Drive with W A S D, Space for nitro, Shift to drift, and Esc to stop.

Accent color

Highlights in Car Mode: buttons, switches, the new tab and the start screen.

Themes

Pick a theme and Car Mode restyles instantly. Chrome doesn't let extensions recolor its own tab bar automatically, so to theme Chrome's window too, press Also theme Chrome's tab bar and load the folder once.
